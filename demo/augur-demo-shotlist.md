## Recording safety

- Fresh isolated browser context; no copied authentication cookies or session tokens.
- No wallet signing, chain writes, fixture advancement or automatic Showcase cycles.
- Product API writes are blocked during capture. Practice credits stay in the isolated browser's local storage.
- The Privy dialog contains no personal email, account details, or credentials.
- Editorial labels are video overlays, not changes to or fabricated states within the application.
- No terminal, browser chrome, environment files, private keys, health dumps, or external paid generation service is filmed.
